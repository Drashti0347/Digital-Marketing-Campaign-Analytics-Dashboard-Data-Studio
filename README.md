# 📈 Digital Marketing Campaign Analytics Dashboard | Looker Studio

## 📌 Project Overview

Digital marketing campaigns generate massive amounts of data across multiple advertising channels, making it challenging for businesses to monitor campaign performance, optimize budgets, and measure marketing effectiveness. Traditional reports often lack interactivity, making it difficult to identify trends and support data-driven decision-making.

This project presents an **interactive 3-page Digital Marketing Campaign Analytics Dashboard** built in **Data Studio**. The dashboard transforms raw marketing campaign data into meaningful business insights by providing interactive visualizations, calculated KPIs, and dynamic filters to monitor campaign performance, advertising spend, impressions, clicks, and overall marketing effectiveness.

---

# 🎯 Business Objectives

- ✔ Monitor overall digital marketing campaign performance.
- ✔ Analyze campaign budgets, impressions, clicks, and media costs.
- ✔ Compare campaign performance across multiple marketing channels.
- ✔ Identify top-performing advertisers and campaign categories.
- ✔ Evaluate marketing effectiveness using calculated KPIs.
- ✔ Provide an interactive reporting solution for marketing teams and business stakeholders.

# 📊 Calculated Fields Created

## 📈 Average Click Through Rate (CTR)

```
CTR = (Clicks / Impressions) × 100
```

Measures the percentage of users who clicked on advertisements after viewing them.

---

## 💰 Average Cost Per Click (CPC)

```
CPC = Media Cost / Clicks
```

Calculates the average advertising cost incurred for each click.
